WHAT PEOPLE ASSUME
"A bonus issue is free shares, so I'm better off."
WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENS
A 5% bonus adjusts the price down ~4.8% and gives you 5% more shares. The screen looks cheaper overnight; your position is unchanged.
