- ✅ **`feed:*` incident dedupe** (`20260718105401_fix_feed_status_sweep_incident_dedup`, applied live 2026-07-18): closes the audit-2026-07-15 open item (a), the last `feed:*` half of the false-incident cleanup. `ingest.sweep_feed_status` edge-triggered both incident rules on the previously **stored** state, but the machine writes `closed` at every market close — and `closed` means "no information", not "the feed changed".
